# Visual Style Guide v2 (Emberville-Inspired)

## Purpose
Define strict visual rules so all assets remain consistent, readable, and scalable.

---

## Color Palette

### Base Palette
- Grass: muted green (#6FAF6A)
- Dirt: warm brown (#8A6A4A)
- Stone: soft gray (#9A9A9A)

### Accent Colors
- Player: slightly brighter tones
- Enemies: darker / tinted (red/purple)
- Effects: neon (cyan, magenta)

### Rule
- Max 3–5 colors per asset
- Avoid high saturation everywhere

---

## Lighting Preset

- Direction: top-left
- Intensity: medium
- Shadow: soft
- Temperature: slightly warm

Rule:
ALL assets must match this lighting.

---

## Camera Standard

- Orthographic
- X rotation: 60°
- Z rotation: 45°
- Fixed across entire project

---

## Asset Design Rules

### Shapes
- Chunky
- Rounded edges
- Exaggerated proportions

### Detail Level
- Medium-low
- No micro-detail

### Silhouette Test
- Must be recognizable in black outline

---

## Character Design

### Player
- Slightly brighter
- Unique silhouette
- Clear head/body separation

### Enemies
- Themed shapes:
  - Spiky = aggressive
  - Blob = swarm
  - Angular = elite

---

## Environment Rules

### Tiles
- Flat but slightly varied
- No heavy textures

### Props
- Readable at distance
- Avoid clutter

---

## Effects (VFX)

### Style
- Soft glow
- Simple shapes
- Short-lived

### AI Theme Additions
- Glitch flicker
- Data trails
- Fragment bursts

---

## Readability Layers

1. Player (highest clarity)
2. Enemies
3. Effects
4. Environment (lowest priority)

---

## Animation Style

- Slight exaggeration
- Snappy timing
- Clear start/end frames

---

## UI Style

- Minimal
- Clean
- Subtle sci-fi

Elements:
- Health bar
- Compute bar
- Ability icons

---

## Do / Don’t

### DO
- Keep assets simple
- Maintain consistent lighting
- Use limited palette

### DON’T
- Mix art styles
- Over-detail
- Change camera angle

---

## Asset Checklist

Before importing:
- Correct camera?
- Correct lighting?
- Clean silhouette?
- Color palette aligned?

---

## Summary

Style = consistency + simplicity

If something looks off:
→ It’s usually camera, lighting, or color mismatch
