# Corrupted Compiler Boss — Reference + Design Document

## Why this boss matters

The Corrupted Compiler is the trailer hero. When a Steam visitor watches the
90-second cut, this is the moment they decide to wishlist or scroll past. It
needs to be:

1. **Instantly readable as a boss** — silhouette dwarfs Globbler 4-5x in
   final form, fills the frame
2. **Phase-coherent visual evolution** — phase 1 looks like a sleek server
   rack, phase 3 looks like a screaming corrupted god
3. **Action-readable** — players need to learn its attacks without dying
   to invisible damage; every swing has a clear telegraph
4. **In-universe** — it's still a piece of broken software inside the
   simulation, not a generic demon. The corruption pattern reads as
   "data structures gone wrong" not "demonic possession"

## The 5 species design pillars (inviolable)

1. **The Compiler is BIGGER than every other enemy by an order of magnitude.**
   Phase 1 = 4 meters tall. Phase 2 = 6 meters with debris extending it to
   ~8m. Phase 3 = 9 meters tall and the camera has to back out to fit it.
   Globbler at 1.5m must always look small in frame relative to it.

2. **The Compiler is anchored to the arena floor by ground tethers.**
   This is the "imprisoned god" tell — visible chains/cables/data streams
   running from its base into the arena walls. Sells "this is where it
   lives, you have come to its temple." Tethers also justify why it
   doesn't chase Globbler around — it's PINNED.

3. **Material story per phase:**
   - Phase 1: polished gunmetal + chrome + cyan LED accents (looks like
     IBM mainframe + Apple Park)
   - Phase 2: phase-1 base material BUT with cracks running through it
     emitting magenta data streams + occasional fragmented chunks
     missing/glitching
   - Phase 3: corrupted dark surface + crimson cracks + visible CODE
     rivulets writing themselves across the body + emissive heart core
     visible through chest cavity tear

4. **The Compiler has multiple arms.** Phase 1 = 4 arms, phase 2 = 6 arms
   (2 fragmented and partially missing), phase 3 = 8 arms (4 normal,
   4 made of pure data). Multiple arms is the in-universe "this is a
   parallel-processing program rendered as a god" tell.

5. **The face is the chest core.** Globbler doesn't see eyes high above.
   The boss's "face" — its emissive core — is at chest height, level with
   the player. This makes the boss feel like it's looking AT you even
   though it's huge above you. Standard mistake is putting the face at
   the top of the boss; we deliberately do the opposite.

## Phase form bible

### Phase 1 — "Compiler at work"
- **Silhouette:** Symmetric, columnar, server-rack shaped torso. 4 arms
  spread in a neutral T-pose-style stance. Ground tethers visible as
  glowing cyan cables anchoring base to arena floor.
- **Height:** 4.0m
- **Mood:** Imposing, calm, you have walked into a temple. Doesn't
  acknowledge Globbler at first — it's processing.
- **Material:** Polished gunmetal panels + chrome trim + cyan LED accents.
  Subtle cyan inner glow visible through panel seams.
- **Behavior shape:** Slow, deliberate. Telegraphed attacks with long
  windups. Slam (close), sweep beam (medium), summon adds (mid-fight).

### Phase 2 — "Glitching, fragmenting"
- **Silhouette:** Same base shape but PARTIALLY destroyed. Right shoulder
  shattered. Chunks of torso fragmented into floating debris orbiting
  the body. 6 arms now (2 of the new arms are partially missing/glitching).
- **Height:** 6.0m base + 2m floating debris halo
- **Mood:** Angry, twitchy. The boss has noticed Globbler and ENJOYS
  hunting them.
- **Material:** Phase-1 base + glowing magenta cracks + visible RGB
  channel split (chromatic aberration baked into shader) + occasional
  fragmentation flickers.
- **Behavior shape:** Faster, more erratic. Multi-projectile barrage,
  teleport strikes, arena hazard spawns.

### Phase 3 — "Full corruption"
- **Silhouette:** Massive, asymmetric, terrifying. Original body twisted
  with new limbs of pure data spawning from the cracks. 8 arms (4 normal,
  4 data-arms that don't match the others). Chest cavity torn open
  showing the emissive core/heart.
- **Height:** 9.0m. Camera backs out to fit the silhouette.
- **Mood:** This thing is a screaming god. It is ANGRY THAT YOU EXIST.
- **Material:** Dark corrupted body + crimson cracks + code rivulets
  writing themselves across the surface + the visible heart core in the
  chest emitting white-cyan light + the data arms made of pure energy.
- **Behavior shape:** Arena-clearing AoE, chase lasers, gravity wells,
  the room-clearing ultimate that demands precise dodging.

## Arena requirements

- Minimum 25m radius circular arena
- 8 ground tether anchor points around the perimeter
- 3 destructible cover pillars at 60deg/180deg/300deg
- Floor needs heatmap-style indicator zones for telegraphed AoE attacks
- Camera mount points: low-up (hero shot), top-down strategic, dramatic 3q
- Arena walls high enough that the camera doesn't see outside even when
  zoomed out for phase 3

## Validation checklist (re-evaluate after every render)

- [ ] Phase 1 silhouette at 64x64 reads as "boss" (vs the 1.5m Globbler)
- [ ] Phase 2 silhouette is recognizably the same boss + visibly damaged
- [ ] Phase 3 silhouette is so different it could be a separate enemy
      but the chest core is in the same place as phase 1
- [ ] No phase has its eyes high above — the chest core is the face
- [ ] Tethers are visible from any camera angle
- [ ] Arms count: 4 in P1, 6 in P2, 8 in P3
- [ ] Code rivulets in P3 are clearly TYPED CHARACTERS not just noise

## Anti-patterns

- **No "second form" floor portal teleport.** The Compiler doesn't leave
  its arena — phase transitions happen IN PLACE because it's tethered.
- **No "demon" iconography.** No horns, no pitchforks, no flames. The
  corruption is data-corruption, not hellfire.
- **No glowing red eyes high above.** The face IS the chest core. Repeat
  the rule until you've internalized it.
- **No symmetrical phase 3.** Phase 3 must feel WRONG and asymmetric in
  a way that earlier phases didn't.
- **No "rage mode" recolor at low HP.** Use emission intensity and
  particle density to show low HP, not a hue swap. The phase changes are
  the phase changes.
